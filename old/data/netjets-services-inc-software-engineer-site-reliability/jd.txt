Purpose of Position
Are you a curious and passionate engineer who desires to drive innovation in software and applications or data solutions for the undisputed leader in private aviation? Do you want a high level of ownership in solving complex problems? Does the challenge of designing software or data solutions  that leverage groundbreaking automation for airline operations, customer experience and logistics appeal to you?  If so we are excited to have you join our software engineering ranks.

As part of our Site Reliability Engineering team, you will design, build, and optimize systems that power the leader in private aviation—ensuring high availability, performance, and efficiency across airline operations, customer experience, and logistics platforms. If you're motivated by solving large-scale reliability challenges, reducing toil through automation, and continuously improving system health, we're excited to have you on our team.

Tasks and Responsibilities
Drive the design and implementation of reliable, scalable software solutions, ensuring new features and enhancements meet availability, resilience, and performance standards.
Partner with cross-functional teams—including architecture, infrastructure operations, cybersecurity, and data—to design and implement systems that balance business functionality with operational reliability and risk mitigation.
Build and maintain automated CI/CD pipelines to enable consistent, repeatable, and efficient build, test, and deployment processes.
Embed quality and security into the software lifecycle by implementing automated testing, code quality checks, vulnerability scanning, and pipeline enforcement (e.g., unit test coverage, static analysis, and compliance checks).
Develop and enhance observability and operational practices (monitoring, alerting, logging) to proactively detect and respond to system issues.
Work within an agile environment to iteratively deliver high-impact improvements while continuously improving system reliability, performance, and operational efficiency.
